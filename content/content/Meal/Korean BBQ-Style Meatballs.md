# [Korean BBQ-Style Meatballs](https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1019763-korean-bbq-style-meatballs)

![Korean BBQ-Style Meatballs](https://static01.nyt.com/images/2023/04/19/multimedia/19kidsrex-meatballs-gvjq/19kidsrex-meatballs-gvjq-videoSixteenByNineJumbo1600-v2.jpg)

## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 pound ground beef (round or chuck) 
- [ ] ½ cup finely crushed Ritz crackers (12 crackers) 
- [ ] ½ cup chopped scallions 
- [ ] 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce 
- [ ] 2 tablespoons minced garlic 
- [ ] 1 teaspoon kosher salt (such as Diamond Crystal) 
- [ ] 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper 

## Instructions
- [ ] Heat oven to 425 degrees. In a large bowl, combine all of the ingredients and use your hands to gently mix.
- [ ] Shape the meat into 12 golf-ball-size rounds (about 2 inches in diameter), and arrange on a greased rimmed baking sheet.
- [ ] Bake until golden and cooked through, about 15 minutes. Serve warm.
