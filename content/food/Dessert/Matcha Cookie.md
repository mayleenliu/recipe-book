# Brown Butter Matcha Cookie


## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 cups all purpose flour
- [ ] 1 tsp baking soda
- [ ] ½ tsp salt
- [ ] 5 tsp matcha powder
- [ ] 1 cup [](https://amzn.to/3tJLMbX)unsalted butter
- [ ] ½ cup granulated sugar
- [ ] 1 cup light brown sugar
- [ ] 1 tsp vanilla extract
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] < 1/2 cup white chocolate chips
## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
- [ ] Brown the butter.
- [ ] Combine flour, baking soda, salt, and matcha in a bowl.
- [ ] Mix white sugar and brown sugar to your butter. Once well combined, add in your eggs and vanilla.
- [ ] Mix dry with wet ingredients.
- [ ] Add in the white chocolate chips and mix until they are well incorporated.
- [ ] Use a cookie scoop to portion out dough and let rest in the fridge for 30-35 minutes.
- [ ] Cover 2 baking sheets with parchment paper and put cookies on.
- [ ] Bake for 9 minutes at 350 F.
- [ ] Let cookies cool for 10 minutes before putting them on a cooling rack.
