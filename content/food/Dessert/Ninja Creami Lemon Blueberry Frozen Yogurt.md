# [Ninja Creami Lemon Blueberry Frozen Yogurt](https://www.foodbanjo.com/ninja-creami-lemon-blueberry-frozen-yogurt/#recipe)

![Ninja Creami Lemon Blueberry Frozen Yogurt](https://www.foodbanjo.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ninja-creami-lemon-blueberry-yogurt-1200fp.jpg)

## Ingredients
- [ ] 3/4 cup Greek yogurt
- [ ] 1/2 cup milk
- [ ] 1/3 cup lemon curd
- [ ] 2 Tbsp honey
- [ ] 1/2 cup blueberries

## Instructions
- [ ] Add yogurt, milk, lemon curd, and honey to Ninja Creami container. Stir to combine.
- [ ] Add blueberries and then lightly push down so they are submerged.
- [ ] Freeze for at least 18 hours on a level surface in your freezer.
- [ ] Remove, and process on the Light Ice Cream or Frozen Yogurt setting. If needed, respin or add a splash of milk and respin.
