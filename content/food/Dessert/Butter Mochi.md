Butter Mochi


## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 lb mochiko (Japanese glutinous rice flour)
- [ ] 2 cups sugar (reduced from 3)
- [ ] 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- [ ] 4 eggs, beaten
- [ ] 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- [ ] 1/2 cup butter, melted
- [ ] 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
- [ ] 1 can (14.5 oz) evaporated milk
## Instructions
- [ ] Preheat electric oven to 350°F.
- [ ] Grease a 13 x 9 x 2-inch pan or two 8 x 8 x 2-inch pans.
- [ ] In a large bowl, combine mochiko, sugar, and baking powder.
- [ ] Add remaining ingredients and mix well with a wire whisk.
- [ ] Pour into prepared pan(s) and bake for 50 to 60 minutes then let cool.
Makes 24 servings.
