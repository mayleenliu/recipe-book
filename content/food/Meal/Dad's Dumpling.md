# Dumpling 



## Ingredients
- [ ] 1 lb ground pork
- [ ] 2 scallions chopped
- [ ] optional: black fungus mushroom
- [ ] cilantro (bunch)
- [ ] < 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp soy sauce
- [ ] 1 tbsp sesame oil
- [ ] 1 blob ginger chopped
- [ ] 1 pound napa cabbage + pinches of salt

## Instructions
- [ ] Mix together meat and sauces.
- [ ] Cut the vegetables and add them in before wrapping dumplings.
- [ ] Boil the water and drop in dumplings.
- [ ] Add cold water when the pot overboils, 3 times.
