# Ingredients

Dough 
- [ ] 470g warm water (~100°F)
- [ ] 2 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- [ ] 7g active yeast
- [ ] 560g bread flour (>12% protein)

Baking
- [ ] Extra virgin olive oil
	- [ ] 3 tbsp for pan
	- [ ] Few tbsp for drizzling
- [ ] Flaky salt
- [ ] Toppings
	- [ ] Rosemary
	- [ ] Cherry tomatoes

# Day Before

1. Whisk the warm water, salt, olive oil, and yeast in a large bowl. Let sit for 5-10 minutes until it looks foamy or bubbly.
2. Add the flour into the mixture. Mix well with wooden spoon. The dough will be wet and sticky.
3. Scrape down the sides of the bowl.
4. Cover the bowl and let sit for 30 minutes.
5. First fold. Wet hands to "stretch and fold" the dough. Pick a corner, lift the dough, and stretch it to the other side. Rotate the bowl 90°. Perform 4 folds total.
6. Cover the bowl and let sit for 30 minutes.
7. Second fold. Wet hands to "stretch and fold" the dough. Pick a corner, lift the dough, and stretch it to the other side. Rotate the bowl 90°. Perform 4 folds total.
8. Cover the bowl and let sit for 30 minutes.
9. Third fold. Wet hands to "stretch and fold" the dough. Pick a corner, lift the dough, and stretch it to the other side. Rotate the bowl 90°. Perform 4 folds total.
10. Cover the bowl and let sit for 30 minutes.
11. Fourth fold. Wet hands to "stretch and fold" the dough. Pick a corner, lift the dough, and stretch it to the other side. Rotate the bowl 90°. Perform 4 folds total.
12. Cover tightly with plastic wrap and leave in refrigerator overnight.

# Day of Baking

1. Pull the dough from the refrigerator. Let it warm to room temperature.
2. Fold the dough. Wet hands to "stretch and fold" the dough. Pick a corner, lift the dough, and stretch it to the other side. Rotate the bowl 90°. Perform 4 folds total.
3. Generously grease a 9x13 pan with 3 tbsp olive oil. Focaccia loves olive oil!
4. Place the dough into the pan. Gentle pull towards corners, but not all the way. Drizzle olive oil on top.
5. Let rise for ~1 hour. Cover the pan completely with a towel.
6. Preheat the oven to 425°F convection.
7. Drizzle the dough with more olive oil.
8. Oil your hands to dimple and jiggle the focaccia.
9. Add whatever toppings you'd like. Oil toppings to prevent burning.
10. Bake the focaccia for 20-22 minutes.
