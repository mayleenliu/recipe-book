# Star Bread  

## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 cups flour
- [ ] 1/2 cup milk (mix cold milk with hot water to make warm)
- [ ] 1/2 to 2/3 cup hot water
- [ ] 1/4 cup oil OR 4 tablespoon butter
- [ ] 2 tablespoon sugar
- [ ] 1 1/4 tsp salt
- [ ] 2 teaspoon yeast
- garlic mix: 1 tbsp butter, lots of garlic cloves, same ratio garlic to green onion, salt
- cinnamon mix: 3/4 tbsp cinnamon, 3 tbsp sugar

## Instructions
- [ ] Knead till smooth
- [ ] Rise 1 to 2 hours
- [ ] Shape and out into pan
- [ ] Split dough into 4 pieces, roll out flat circles, layer with flavor mix.
- [ ] Put a cup in the center, then cut into 16 slices. Twist the two adjacent sides away from each other and press edges together before tucking under loaf to make 8 petals.
- [ ] Cover and rise 1 hr
- [ ] Bake at 350 F for 25 min
