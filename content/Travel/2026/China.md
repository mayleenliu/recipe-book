##  Overview

```
    SEA --> Shanghai --> Zhangjiajie --> Chongqing --> Chengdu --> Jiuzhaigou --> Shanghai --> SEA
```

- **Zhangjiajie:** 3 Days (2 nights Wulingyuan / 1 night City Center)
- **Chongqing:** 2 Days
- **Chengdu:** 3 Days (Include Leshan day trip)
- **Jiuzhaigou:** 3 Days    

> [!TIP] Regional Transit Rules & Food Tips
> 
> - **Food Spice Warning:** 粤菜 (Cantonese / Guangdong food) is non-spicy. Chongqing/Sichuan is extremely spicy—always specify **不辣 (Bù là / No spice)**.
>     
>     - _Recommended non-spicy broths:_ Tomato broth (番茄锅) or White Sugar broth (白糖锅).
>         
> - **Navigation Advice:**
>     
>     - **Chongqing:** Hard to walk (hilly). Call **DiDi / Taxis** everywhere.
>         
>     - **Chengdu:** High traffic. Prefer **Metro / Trains** over taxis.
>         

## 📅 Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary

### Day 1: Friday, Oct 17 — Fly Out

- [ ] **3:40 PM:** Flight departs from Seattle (SEA).

### Day 2: Saturday, Oct 18 — Arrival in Shanghai

- [ ] **7:50 PM:** Land at Shanghai Pudong (PVG).
- [ ] **Hotel:** Check into **上海大众空港宾馆 (Dazhong Airport Hotel)**. Early sleep.

### Day 3: Sunday, Oct 19 — Shanghai ➔ Zhangjiajie (Low Valleys)

- [ ] **04:45 AM:** Wake up & head to Domestic Terminal.
- [ ] **05:10 AM:** Check bags & go through security.
- [ ] **06:10 AM:** Board flight.
- [ ] **06:40 AM:** Fly out of PVG ➔ Land in Zhangjiajie (~09:30 AM).
- [ ] **10:30 AM:** Forward luggage from airport to Wulingyuan hotel (or bring to South Gate lockers).
- [ ] **11:30 AM – 02:30 PM:** Taxi to **South Entrance**. Hike **Golden Whip Stream** (see wild monkeys), then loop back out.
- [ ] **02:30 PM – 03:30 PM:** Taxi/shuttle to **Wulingyuan (East Entrance)** & check into hotel.
- [ ] **03:30 PM – 05:30 PM:** Enter East Entrance ➔ Park bus to **Ten-Mile Natural Gallery** ➔ Ride mini-train.
- 🏨 **Hotel:** Wulingyuan Town (Night 1).

### Day 4: Monday, Oct 20 — Zhangjiajie (Avatar Peaks)

- [ ] **08:30 AM – 05:00 PM:** Enter via East Entrance.
    - [ ] Shuttle to **Bailong Elevator** to explore **Yuanjiajie** (_Hallelujah Mountain, First Bridge Under Heaven, Enchanting Terrace_).
    - [ ] Mountain bus to **Tianzi Mountain**.
    - [ ] Ride **Tianzi Mountain Cable Car** down; exit East Entrance.
- 🏨 **Hotel:** Wulingyuan Town (Night 2).

### Day 5: Tuesday, Oct 21 — Grand Canyon Glass Bridge ➔ City Transfer

- [ ] **08:30 AM – 01:30 PM:** Taxi to **Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon**.
    - [ ] Use **Line B Ticket** for Glass Bridge, gorge hike, stone slides, & lake boat.
- [ ] **Afternoon:** Return to Wulingyuan, retrieve bags, and taxi (45 mins) to Zhangjiajie City Center.
- 🏨 **Hotel:** Zhangjiajie City Center (Night 3 - _Positioned near Tianmen Cableway_).

### Day 6: Wednesday, Oct 22 — Tianmen Mountain ➔ High-Speed Train to Chongqing

- [ ] **07:30 AM:** Check out & store luggage in lockers at Tianmen Mountain Cableway station.
- [ ] **08:00 AM – 01:30 PM:** **Tianmen Mountain (Line A Route)**:
    - [ ] World's longest cable car up.
    - [ ] Cliffside glass skywalks & peak trails.
    - [ ] Escalators down to **Heaven's Gate Cave** & 999 steps.
- [ ] **02:00 PM:** Grab luggage; taxi (15 mins) to **Zhangjiajie West Railway Station**
- [ ] **Late Afternoon:** High-speed train to **Chongqing** (~3 hours).
- 🏨 **Hotel:** Jiefangbei area, Chongqing.

### Day 7: Thursday, Oct 23 — Chongqing

> [!NOTE] Transportation Strategy
> 
> Call **DiDi** for transport today; walking routes can be confusing due to elevation layers.

- [ ] Breakfast.
- [ ] Ride **Huangguan Escalator** (皇冠大扶梯) if not a major detour.
- [ ] View train passing through building at **Liziba Station (李子坝)**.
- [ ] Walk & shop at **Eling Testbed2 (鹅岭二厂)**.
- [ ] Explore & shop around **Jiefangbei (解放碑)**.
- [ ] **Bayi Road Food Street (八一路好吃街):**
    - [ ] Chongqing noodles
    - [ ] Sour & spicy noodles (酸辣粉)
    - [ ] Glutinous rice balls (汤圆)
    - [ ] Iced jelly (冰粉)
    - [ ] Grilled skewers
- [ ] **Evening:** **Two Rivers Night Boat Tour** departing from _Chaotianmen Pier 7_.
- 🍲 **Local Food Checklist:** Old Hotpot (老火锅 - specify 白糖/番茄锅), Chongqing Cold Noodles (重庆凉面), Pickled Radish (酸萝卜), Old Duck Soup (老鸭汤), Douhua Rice (豆花饭), Hu's Pig Trotter Soup (胡记蹄花汤).
    

### Day 8: Friday, Oct 24 — Chongqing ➔ Chengdu

- [ ] Travel from Chongqing to Chengdu via train.
- [ ] Check in at hotel (Recommended area: **Taikoo Li**).
- [ ] _Optional:_ Get a local haircut!
- [ ] Sights & Relaxation Options:
    - [ ] Wuhou Shrine
    - [ ] People’s Park (Tea house relaxation)
    - [ ] Chengdu Panda Base
    - [ ] Qingcheng Mountain _(if time permits)_
- 🍲 **Chengdu Foods to Try:** Mapo Tofu, Dan Dan Noodles, Chuan Chuan, Sichuan Hot Pot, Sweet Water Noodles, Zhong Dumplings. _(Tip: Look for busy, crowded restaurants!)_

### Day 9: Saturday, Oct 25 — Day Trip: Leshan Giant Buddha

- [ ] **Before 07:30 AM:** Depart hotel.
- [ ] **Train:** Chengdu Metro Line 2 ➔ Chengdu East Station (成都东站) ➔ Bullet train to Leshan (45–50 min).
- [ ] **Transit:** 30-min taxi to Baxiandong Pier (八仙洞码头).
- [ ] **Boat Tour:** Check fog/weather conditions, buy boat ticket on the spot to view the **Leshan Giant Buddha**.
- [ ] **Afternoon:** Taxi into Leshan city to explore and eat.
- 🍲 **Leshan Food Specialties:**
    - [ ] Qiaojiao Beef (乐山跷脚牛肉)
    - [ ] Sweet-skinned Duck (甜皮鸭)
    - [ ] Egg-baked Cake (蛋烘糕)
- [ ] Return to Chengdu by train.

### Day 10: Sunday, Oct 26 — Chengdu

- [ ] Free day to explore Chengdu markets, food scene, and cultural sites.

### Day 11: Monday, Oct 27 — Chengdu ➔ Jiuzhaigou
- [ ] **Bullet Train:** Chengdu ➔ Huanglong-Jiuzhai Station (黄龙九寨站) _(1 hr 39 min)_.
- [ ] **Public Bus:** Station ➔ Jiuzhaigou _(1.5 – 2 hours)_.
- [ ] **~02:00 PM:** Arrive in Jiuzhaigou.
- 🏨 **Hotel:** Stay in Park Entrance area (风景区沟口).
    - _Required feature:_ Must have central heating/heaters (temps near freezing).
    - _Recommended:_ 便便捷亚朵 or 旅游中心亚朵酒店.

### Day 12: Tuesday, Oct 28 — Jiuzhaigou Valley (Rize Valley Route)

> [!MAP] [Jiuzhaigou Park Tourist Map](https://www.chinadiscovery.com/assets/images/travel-guide/maps/jiuzhaigou-valley-tourist-map.jpg)

- [ ] **Rize Valley (日则沟):** Take park bus up to Five Flower Lake (五花海), Mirror Lake (静海), Arrow Bamboo Lake (箭竹海), Panda Lake (熊猫海), and Pearl Shoal Waterfall (珍珠滩瀑布).
- [ ] **Lunch:** Nuorilang Visitor Center.
- [ ] Visit **Nuorilang Waterfall (诺日朗瀑布)** and explore the southern valley branch.
- 🍲 **Dinner:** Tibetan Yak Meat Hotpot (藏式牦牛汤).

### Day 13: Wednesday, Oct 29 — Jiuzhaigou Valley (Zezhawa Valley Route)
- [ ] **Zezhawa Valley (则查洼沟):** Long Lake (长海) & Five-Color Pond (五彩池).
- [ ] **Lunch / Afternoon:** Luneng Resort area (鲁能胜地) for walking and souvenir shopping.

### Day 14: Thursday, Oct 30 — Jiuzhaigou ➔ Shanghai
- [ ] Travel from Jiuzhaigou back to Shanghai.
- 🏨 **Hotel:** Shanghai.

### Day 15: Friday, Oct 31 — Fly Home
- [ ] **05:55 PM:** Flight departs Shanghai (PVG).
- [ ] **01:30 PM (Same Day):** Land in Seattle (SEA).
