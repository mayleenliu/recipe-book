> [!INFO] Antoni Gaudí (1852–1926) — "God's Architect"
> * **Movement:** *Modernisme* (Catalan Modernism / Art Nouveau)[cite: 2].
> * **Key Traits:** Detail-oriented, heavily influenced by nature, architecture, and religion[cite: 2].
> * **Craftsmanship:** Worked with ceramics, stained glass, wrought ironwork, and carpentry[cite: 2].
> * **Techniques:** Pioneered *trencadís* (using waste ceramic pieces)[cite: 2].

> [!INFO] The Block of Discord (*Illa de la Discòrdia*)
> Five adjacent houses on Passeig de Gràcia built in clashing architectural styles[cite: 2]:
> 1. **Casa Lleó Morera** | Pg. de Gràcia 35 | Lluís Domènech i Montaner (1905)[cite: 2]
> 2. **Casa Mulleras** | Pg. de Gràcia 37 | Enric Sagnier (1906)[cite: 2]
> 3. **Casa Bonet** | Pg. de Gràcia 39 | Marceliano Coquillat (1901/1915)[cite: 2]
> 4. **Casa Amatller** | Pg. de Gràcia 41 | Josep Puig i Cadafalch (1900)[cite: 2]
> 5. **Casa Batlló** | Pg. de Gràcia 43 | Antoni Gaudí (1877/1906)[cite: 2]
