Mari's list below, Kailua was the beach town :) Also this list is from 2017 so I'm sure lots has changed, but good starting point. 

**FAVORITES**

•         **Poke:** Alicia’s Market, Da Hawaiian Poke Co., Maguro Brothers, Fort Ruger Market (hole in the wall but popular amongst locals – you can walk here (~15 min) from Diamond Head … then go to Monsarrat Shave Ice right after for dessert (also ~15 min), Foodland (lots of options but not the best; still good though)

•         **Ramen/Noodles:** Marukame Udon, Tsujita Ramen, Menya Musashi Ramen, Gomaichi Ramen

•         **Local/Hawaiian:** Helena’s, Young’s Fish Market, Rainbow Drive Inn (this restaurant has been open for 50+ yrs … if you don’t know what to get the mixed plate is a good option), Mitsuken, Sugoi, Diamond Head Market & Bakery, Shirokiya Village Walk (like a depachika in Japan)

•         **Sweet Treats:** Pipeline Bakery & Creamery, Kamehameha Bakery (poi malasadas), Leonard’s Bakery (malasadas), Watanabe Bakery, Liliha Bakery, Bubbie’s (mocha ice cream)

o   **Shave Ice:** Monsarrat Shave Ice (this is a small outdoor shave ice spot … the best shave ice I tried in Oahu imo), Shimazu, Uncle Clay’s House of Pure Aloha, Waiola Shave Ice (this place rly gained its popularity after Obama decided to pop in a few years back. They even created a shave ice under his name! I would still go with Monsarrat though over this)

•         **Restaurants (Breakfast & Brunch):** Koko Head Café, Sweet E’s Café, Egghead Café (located in the middle of an industrial area of HNL / can seem sketchy but the brunch is worth it!!!!!! We ordered the pancake sampler to try the diff flavors as well as the salmon eggs benedict and the bao bowl with a sunny side up egg … also try their macadamia nut iced latte – very delish) , Café Kaila, Arvo, Bill’s Waikiki

o   **Acai Bowls:** Diamond Head Cove Health Bar, Blue Hawaii, Island Vintage Coffee (I went here every day for the iced mac nut latte – SO good. The coffee is pretty strong too. Their acai bowls are so fresh and scrumptious as well. They have a location near the Royal Hawaiian / Sheraton Waikiki)

•         **Restaurants (Lunch & Dinner):** Senia (Great spot for a fancier dinner – need to make a res), Pig & the Lady (I love this place. We had dinner here twice in 1 week. The chicken wing app is their top seller and is very good. Ask any local and they will prob recommend this spot as well! Make a res!), MW, Sushi Izakaya Gaku, Chef Mavro, 12_th_ Ave Grill, Nobu (not that it’s that different from other Nobu restaurants, but it’s a fun space)

•         **Bars:** Bar Leather Apron (need reservation), Tchin Tchin, The Manifest, Pint & Jigger, Mai Tai’s (Ala Moana – super local at night), The Study @ The Modern Honolulu, Buho Cucina y Cantina (haven’t been yet but my friends go there) – I would also add Sky Waikiki Rooftop … nothing too crazy but has nice views of Honolulu from above

I probably missed a bunch of places, but that’s all I got right now. Frolic Hawaii’s Instagram account (@frolichawaii (Agreed!! Follow them!)) posts a lot of food pics that look great, if you need ideas, too!

Neighborhoods with fun restaurants/bars and cute boutiques: Chinatown, Ward Village, SALT in Kakaako, Kailua.

I recommend renting a car for a day and driving around the island – at least up to the North Shore. Honolulu is very urban and cosmopolitan, whereas the North Shore is country. It almost feels like you’re in a completely different place when you’re up there. If you do, here are some fun stops along the way:

•         Pali Lookout

•         Kailua/Lanikai – beach town with cute shops and restaurants and some of the best known beaches on the island

•         Kaneohe Sand Bar (Book a tour before you go – I didn’t have time to try this but looks beautiful – [click here](https://www.google.com/search?q=kaneohe+sandbar&source=lnms&tbm=isch&sa=X&ved=0ahUKEwiVv9eesvLUAhULh1QKHdQ1BxoQ_AUIBygC&biw=1920&bih=947))

•         Kualoa Ranch/Chinaman’s Hat

•         Shrimp Trucks in Kahuku, Kahuku Superette for poke, Ted’s Bakery near Sunset Beach – I would also add Giovanni’s Shrimp Truck located in the North Shore. If you want garlic-y goodness this is for you!

•         Surf spots & beaches: Waimea Bay, Pipeline, etc.

•         Haleiwa Town
