# Kombucha  

## Ingredients
- [ ] 3 tbsp loose leaf tea
- [ ] 14 cups water (3.4 L)
- [ ] 0.75 - 1 cup sugar
- [ ] 2 cup starter liquid
- [ ] Pellicle

## Instructions
- [ ] Steep tea in water and add sugar.
- [ ] Let cool to room temperature.
- [ ] Pour starter liquid and pellicle into jar.
- [ ] Cover for 7-14 days.
- [ ] Taste after a week, should be sour and not too sweet.
- [ ] Remove 2 cups liquid and the pellicles into a separate container.
- [ ] Add flavors to airtight bottles and pour in kombucha, leaving some room at top.
- [ ] Leave in room temperature for 3-7 days, depending on temperature.
- [ ] Put bottles in fridge to stop fermentation.
